So, what is GTM?

Go-to-market, or GTM, is how a company finds the right customers and gets its product in front of them. That means figuring out:

  • Who should we sell to?
  • How do we find them?
  • What do they care about?
  • How do we reach them?

A lot of careers work on different parts of that problem.

You don't need to know which one you want to do. They all involve some combination of understanding customers, using data, researching markets, and figuring out how to grow a business.

The work is changing.

Before

  • Research companies one at a time.
  • Search LinkedIn for the right person.
  • Copy information between tools.
  • Write outreach manually.
  • Repeat everything for the next company.

Now

  • Build a list.
  • Add data automatically.
  • Use AI to research each company.
  • Prioritize the best opportunities.
  • Turn the process into a workflow you can run again.

That's the idea behind GTM engineering. Clay describes it as building automated revenue systems using AI, data, and workflow automation.

Where Clay fits

Clay brings company and people data, AI research, and workflow automation into one place. Instead of just doing the task, you can build the system that does the task.
