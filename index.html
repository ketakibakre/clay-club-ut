What is go-to-market?

Go-to-market, or GTM, is how a company figures out:

  • Who should buy its product
  • How to find those people
  • What to say to them
  • How to turn that into a repeatable process

Sales, marketing, growth, product marketing, revenue operations, and GTM strategy all work on different parts of that problem.

Revenue Operations / GTM Systems

GTM engineering is how companies turn that work into systems.

Instead of having people manually research companies, copy data between tools, and decide who to contact one at a time, GTM engineers build systems that do much of that work automatically.

  1. 01 Choose who to target
  2. 02 Find the companies
  3. 03 Add data
  4. 04 Find the right people
  5. 05 Research and prioritize
  6. 06 Reach out

A GTM engineer might combine company data, AI research, contact information, scoring, and outreach into one repeatable workflow.

This is where Clay comes in. Clay lets you build many of these systems without traditional software development.
