What is GTM?

Go-to-market, or GTM, is how a company figures out who to sell to, how to find them, and how to reach them.

Who should we sell to?

How do we find them?

What do they care about?

How do we reach them?

Sales, growth, marketing, RevOps, product marketing, and GTM engineering all work on different parts of this process.

How GTM is changing

Before

  • Research companies one at a time
  • Search LinkedIn for the right person
  • Copy information between tools
  • Write outreach manually
  • Repeat everything for the next company

Now

  • Build the right list
  • Add data automatically
  • Use AI to research each company
  • Prioritize the best opportunities
  • Build workflows you can reuse

That's where Clay fits.

Clay brings company and people data, AI research, and automation together so teams can build these workflows in one place.
