What is GTM?

How companies find and reach customers.

Go-to-market, or GTM, is how a company decides who to sell to, how to find those people, what they care about, and how to reach them.

Who should we sell to?

How do we find them?

Jobs that work on GTM

Sales

Finds potential customers, talks to them, and helps close deals.

Growth

Tests ways to bring in more customers and grow usage.

Revenue Operations

Manages the tools, data, and processes used by sales and marketing teams.

Product Marketing

Figures out who a product is for and how to explain its value.

GTM Strategy

Helps decide which markets and customers a company should focus on.

GTM Engineering

Uses data, AI, and automation to make GTM work faster and more repeatable.

How GTM is changing

A lot of this work used to be done manually. Now teams can automate more of it.

Before

  • Research companies one at a time
  • Search LinkedIn for the right people
  • Copy information between tools
  • Write outreach one by one
  • Repeat the same work again

Now

  • Build a targeted list
  • Add company and contact data automatically
  • Use AI to research companies
  • Prioritize the best opportunities
  • Reuse the same workflow

Where Clay fits

Clay brings company data, people data, AI research, and automation into one place.

You can use it to build the same kinds of workflows that sales and growth teams use.
